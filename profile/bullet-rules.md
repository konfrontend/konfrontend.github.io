# The Bullet rules

What a Bullet is written by: an agent drafts it from its Story, the person reviews it. Folded from the resumeskills audit, landing place 1 (M1 to M13 in `docs/research/resumeskills-fold-in.md`). The Profile check (`npm run build`) scripts three of them: the opener (rule 2) fails, the number count (rule 7) and the tense (rule 1) warn; the rest are judged by the writer and the reviewer.

## Input

One Story (problem, solution, outcome), the Project's `keywords`, and the Story's canonical Bullets when it has them.

## Output

A canonical Bullet, written under the Story in the Project file and reviewed by the person; or a variant, written into a tailored Version only, carrying the key of a canonical Bullet in the same Story.

## Write

1. One or two rendered lines, past tense, the person as the implied subject (M10).
2. Open with an active verb; never "Responsible for", "Helped", "Assisted", "Participated in", "Worked on" (M1).
3. State a delivered thing and its outcome. A Story with no outcome yields no Bullet; ask for the outcome (M2, M4).
4. Shape: what was achieved, how it is measured, how it was done. Fill each part from the Story; leave a part out when the Story does not fill it, never invent it (M3).
5. No measured outcome: quantify the work item from the Story (components, test cases, tools) (M7). A team Story states the person's own share when the Story records it (M8).
6. A relative figure carries its base: the before value, the period or the sample (M6).
7. At most three numbers; every number appears in the Story (M9, M12).
8. Name the technology the work was built with, spelled as a Keyword of this Project (M11).
9. Strengthen with word choice, never with claims the Story lacks. Omit what does not serve a resume: work later cut, internal product terms, ship dates.
10. A variant starts from a canonical Bullet and shifts the focus to another Fact of the same Story.
11. Confidential figure (`naming: nda`): a true bound or a percentage the Story holds, never an estimate (M13).
