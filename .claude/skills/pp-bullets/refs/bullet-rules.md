# The Bullet rules

What a Bullet is written by (`GLOSSARY.md`: Bullet). `pp-bullets` drafts each Bullet by these rules from the Work repository, the Project's Overview and its Notes; the person reviews it. The Profile check (`npm run build`) scripts part of rules 2 and 3: a duty opener fails, a present-tense opener and more than three numbers warn. The rest is judged by the writer and the reviewer.

1. Shape, in order: what was delivered, its measured effect, how it was done. A part the Work repository, the Overview and the Notes do not fill is left out, never invented.
2. Opens with a past-tense active verb; no duty opener ("Responsible for", "Helped", "Assisted", "Participated in", "Worked on"). One or two rendered lines.
3. A relative figure carries its base (the before value, the period or the sample). At most three numbers.
4. Technology named as this Project's Keyword.
5. Team work states the person's own share when the source shows it.
