A guard line above that starts with `autotriage:` and says `refused` is the answer: print it verbatim and stop; it names the fix.

The Letter is filled, not written: the route's template orders the Slots and caps the Letter, the Slot catalogue says what each holds and how big it may be, the voice rules say how it sounds, and every fact comes from the Profile. This State maps the Job's demands to Slots, fills the Slots in template order, writes `letter.md`, and shows the Letter. `verify` reviews it afterwards; this State applies the rules while filling so `verify` has little to fix. Everything printed here besides the Letter (demand map, fact table) is session output and is thrown away.

Inputs, read all before step 1: `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/job.md` (posting verbatim in the body; `verdict.family` and `verdict.flags` in the frontmatter), the Profile (`profile/profile.md` for `identity`, `terms`, `word_cap`; `profile/projects/*.md`, frontmatter meta and Stories with their keys, as the only source of facts about the freelancer, each Project's `keywords` naming the canonical Skills it proves; `profile/voice.md` for `greeting.<route>`, `sign_off`, `banned_extra`, `allowed_extra`, `cite_role_period`), the Family template `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-<family>.md`, the Slot catalogue `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-slots.md`, the voice rules `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/voice.md`, and the Flag catalogue `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/flags.md` for the `safeguard` and `slot` of each triggered Flag. Output: `letter.md` next to `job.md`. Steps 1 to 8 are the freelance route; a `job.md` with `route: employment` follows the `Employment route` section instead. Contract in `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/candidate-artifacts.md`, schema in `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/schemas/letter.schema.json`.

On the employment route (`route: employment`) the Letter is the cover letter, written to `cover-letter.md`, and the Intro Slot takes the place of the Hook Slot. The Intro Slot starts from the `summary` of the Version `cover-letter.md` names in `version`: `resume/versions/general.md`, or on `version: tailored` `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/version.md`. The guard prints the summary and its source; the Slot puts it in first person and rephrases it to the Job.

## Steps

1. **Demand map.** Walk the whole Job body and list every explicit request the client makes of the proposal (examples, a stack, an approach, availability, subcontracting, a specific question; never a keyword demand, which the Letter ignores). Assign each to the Slot that answers it per the Slot catalogue; what no Slot answers goes to `answers`, in the Job's order, with a header worded from the demand. When the Job lists questions or asks (screening questions, `please include ...`), the Letter is structured by them: hold each as a header, a real question verbatim with numbering stripped, a request fragment reworded in first person from the freelancer's side (never `you`/`your` in a colon header), and note which Slot renders under it (Slot catalogue, `Ask headers`); the question asking for availability is the last block before `closing` and carries `terms`, plus the rate and `billing` when it asks for those. Screening questions (a block introduced as questions to answer when submitting) are demands: map each one. Decide each `when: job asks` Slot of the template from the map: active only with a demand pointing at it. Then add the Safeguards: for every id in `verdict.flags`, look up the Flag; a risk Flag with `slot` other than `none` puts its `safeguard` on the map against that Slot (`plan` is the Family's `plan_slot`). Done when every explicit request and every Safeguard has a Slot, and every `when: job asks` Slot has a decision.

2. **Facts.** For every active Slot that cites the freelancer (`relevant_work`, `gaps`, `stack`, `answers`, `terms`, `billing`), pick the facts from the Projects' frontmatter, Stories, Bullets and Notes (`profile/projects/<slug>.md`) and `profile.md` fields. Choose Projects by match to the Job's requirements, the number the Slot catalogue and template set. Gaps come from the map: a required item of the Job (a must, a required skill, the codebase) that no Project's `keywords` names is a gap, and the nearest Project Story is what is named against it. A preferred or nice-to-have item is never a gap; with no required gap, `gaps` renders nothing. Hours, prices, totals, and timezone overlap are derived from `terms` and `identity` and marked `derived`; `derived` covers arithmetic on Profile numbers only, never a claim about the freelancer (availability, hours reachable, willingness to show code) that no Profile field states. Hold a fact table: fact, Slot, source (`profile/projects/<slug>.md` field, Bullet by its key or Notes line, `profile.md` field, `job.md` for client facts, `derived`). A demand or Slot that needs a fact the Profile lacks (a concrete thing done, a role, a product URL): ask the human for every such fact in one message, naming the Project it would belong to, and wait. A confirmed fact is appended as one list line under `## Notes` of the matching `profile/projects/<slug>.md` with the Edit tool before filling continues; a fact the human denies or does not know is dropped and the Slot is filled without it. Done when every row of the fact table has a source and no question is open.

3. **Fill.** Fill the active Slots in template order, each by its Slot catalogue entry, the template's Slot notes for this Family, and the voice rules. The Hook Slot restates the Job's goal and constraint within the `hook` Slot `cap.chars` and opens with a phrase of understanding; with Flag `mandatory-keyword` triggered, the keyword appears nowhere in the Letter. The plan Slot carries the Safeguards mapped to it, in its own words; `billing` and `answers` carry theirs. When the plan costs more than the posted budget, `billing` states both figures and what the posted budget buys. Done when every active Slot has content or an explicit reason to render nothing, and every mapped demand and Safeguard is stated in its Slot.

4. **Render.** Assemble the plain text: the Profile `greeting.freelance` on line 1, a blank line, then the Slots in template order, one block each, one blank line between blocks. Headers from the Slot catalogue `headers` on their own line with no blank line before the content, replaced by the ask headers from step 1 for the Slots an ask points at, ask blocks in the Job's order after the plan block and the availability ask last, right before `closing`; `follow_on` as the last paragraph of the plan block; `sign_off` alone on the last line. Then read the text against the voice rules once as a whole: prose words under Profile `word_cap` (the template's `cap.prose_words` when unset) with header, list, and `answers` lines excluded, total under the template's `cap.total_words`, characters under its `cap.chars`, every Slot within its Slot catalogue `cap`, one list at most, only `https://` URLs, no banned phrase or pattern, no Markdown. Trim the longest prose Slot first. Done when the text passes that reading.

5. **Write `letter.md`** with the Write tool at `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/letter.md`: frontmatter `slug` (from `job.md`), `family` (from `verdict.family`), `date` (from `job.md`); then the Letter text. The write hook validates the frontmatter against the schema and blocks with the error list on mismatch: fix and write again. Done when the write went through.

6. **Question blocks.** Every question from step 1 is in `letter.md` under its header from step 1; nothing is answered outside `letter.md`. Done when each question has its answer in the file.

7. **Show.** Print, in this order: the demand map, one line per demand as `demand -> Slot`, with the `when: job asks` decisions and the Safeguards placed; the Projects chosen and why, one line each; the fact table. No Letter here: the human sees it only after `verify` has passed it. Done when the three are on screen.

8. **Hand off.** Invoke `route-verify $ARGUMENTS` with the Skill tool, no question in between. Its report ends with the verified Letter. Done when `verify` has run.

## Employment route

The cover letter, from `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-employment.md` (its Slot notes and rule switches) and the Slot catalogue `Employment route` section. The same inputs, less the Family template and the Flag catalogue: no Safeguard has a Slot on this template. Output: the body of `cover-letter.md`, which the `version` State created next to `job.md` with frontmatter only; schema in `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/schemas/cover-letter.schema.json`.

1. **Version.** The guard output above names the Version (`version` in `cover-letter.md`) and prints the summary the `intro` reads, with its source. Done when the summary is in hand.
2. **Demand map.** Walk the Job body for what the cover letter must answer: the requirements `relevant_work` matches, and an explicit ask for gaps (how a missing requirement is covered) or for terms (availability, notice, location, overlap). `gaps` and `terms` are active only with such an ask. Screening questions are form fields, never in the Letter: list them in step 6 and leave them to the human and the Answers in `profile/answers/`. Done when `gaps` and `terms` each have a decision.
3. **Facts.** `intro`: the summary's facts. `relevant_work`: stack and domains that match the Job's requirements, each traced to a Project's `keywords` or Stories; no Project name, no date. A fact the Profile lacks goes to the human as in step 2 of the freelance route. Hold the fact table. Done when every row has a source.
4. **Fill and render.** Fill the active Slots in template order by the Slot catalogue, the template's Slot notes and rule switches, and the voice rules: `intro` is the summary in first person rephrased to the Job's keywords, a role title and `10+ years` allowed there only. Assemble the plain text: the Profile `greeting.employment` on line 1, a blank line, then one prose paragraph per Slot, one blank line between, no headers, no lists, `sign_off` alone on the last line. Count the characters, greeting to sign-off: within the template's `cap.chars`. Trim `relevant_work` first, never `closing` or `sign_off`. Done when the text is within the cap and passes a reading against the voice rules.
5. **Write the body of `cover-letter.md`** at `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/cover-letter.md`: the frontmatter (`slug`, `date`, `version`) stays byte-identical, the Letter text replaces everything after it. The write hook validates the frontmatter: fix and write again on a block. Done when the write went through and the frontmatter is unchanged.
6. **Show.** Print the Version, the `gaps` and `terms` decisions, the screening questions left for the form, and the fact table. No Letter here. Done when the four are on screen.
7. **Hand off.** Invoke `route-verify $ARGUMENTS` with the Skill tool, no question in between. Done when `verify` has run.
