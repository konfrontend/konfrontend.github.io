A guard line above that starts with `autotriage:` and says `refused` is the answer: print it verbatim and stop; it names the fix.

The Letter is filled, not written: the route's template orders the Slots and caps the Letter, the Slot catalogue says what each holds and how big it may be, the voice rules say how it sounds, and every fact comes from the Profile. This State maps the Job's demands to Slots, fills the Slots in template order, writes `letter.md`, and hands off to `verify`. `verify` reviews it afterwards; this State applies the rules while filling so `verify` has little to fix. Everything printed here besides the Letter (demand map, fact table) is session output and is thrown away.

Inputs, read all before step 1: `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/job.md` (posting verbatim in the body; `title`, `requirements` and on the freelance route `flags` in the frontmatter), the Profile (`profile/profile.md` for `identity`, `terms`; `profile/projects/*.md`, frontmatter meta, `## Overview`, `## Bullets` by key and `## Notes`, as the source of facts about the person, each Project's `keywords` naming the canonical Skills it proves; `profile/answers/*.md`, the Answers, a Letter source too; `profile/voice.md` for `greeting.<route>`, `sign_off`, `banned_extra`, `allowed_extra`), the route's template (`$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-freelance.md` or `letter-employment.md`), the Slot catalogue `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-slots.md`, the voice rules `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/voice.md`, and on the freelance route the Flag catalogue `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/flags.md` for the `safeguard` and `slot` of each triggered Flag. Output: `letter.md` next to `job.md`, body only, no frontmatter, on both routes. Steps 1 to 8 are the freelance route; a `job.md` with `route: employment` follows the `Employment route` section instead. Contract in `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/candidate-artifacts.md`.

## Steps

1. **Plan Slot and demand map.** Pick the one plan Slot (`deliverables`, `milestones`, `working_model`) from what the Job asks, by the `Picking the plan Slot` section of `letter-freelance.md`, triggered Flags first; hold the cues matched. Then walk the whole Job body and list every explicit request the client makes of the proposal (examples, a stack, an approach, availability, subcontracting, a specific question; never a keyword demand, which the Letter ignores). Assign each to the Slot that answers it per the Slot catalogue; what no Slot answers goes to `answers`, in the Job's order, with a header worded from the demand. When the Job lists questions or asks (screening questions, `please include ...`), the Letter is structured by them: hold each as a header, a real question verbatim with numbering stripped, a request fragment reworded in first person from the freelancer's side (never `you`/`your` in a colon header), and note which Slot renders under it (Slot catalogue, `Ask headers`); the question asking for availability is the last block before `closing` and carries `terms`, plus the rate and `billing` when it asks for those. Screening questions (a block introduced as questions to answer when submitting) are demands: map each one. Then add the Safeguards: for every id in `flags`, look up the Flag; a risk Flag with `slot` other than `none` puts its `safeguard` on the map against that Slot (`plan` is the plan Slot picked). Done when the plan Slot is picked, every explicit request and every Safeguard has a Slot.

2. **Facts.** For every active Slot that cites the person (`relevant_work`, `answers`, `terms`, `billing`), pick the facts from the Projects' frontmatter, Overviews, Bullets and Notes (`profile/projects/<slug>.md`), the Answers (`profile/answers/<slug>.md`: a question that matches an Answer takes it word for word or tailored) and `profile.md` fields. Choose Projects by match to the Job's `requirements`, the number the Slot catalogue and template set. A required item no Project proves is a Gap: it is never named in the Letter; `relevant_work` may show the nearest thing done as work done, with no mention of the item. Hours, prices, totals, and timezone overlap are derived from `terms` and `identity` and marked `derived`; `derived` covers arithmetic on Profile numbers only, never a claim about the person (availability, hours reachable, willingness to show code) that no Profile field states. Hold a fact table: fact, Slot, source (`profile/projects/<slug>.md` meta field, Overview, Bullet by its key or Notes line; `Answer <slug>`; `profile.md` field; `job.md` for client facts; `derived`). A demand or Slot that needs a fact the Profile lacks (a concrete thing done, a role, a product URL): ask the human for every such fact in one message, naming the Project it would belong to, and wait. A demand for time with a Skill (`years with X`, `how long have you used X`) is such a fact, asked in the same message and never derived from the Projects' `start` and `end`: an open Project and overlapping Projects make that sum wrong. Its answer serves this Letter only and is not appended to `## Notes`. A confirmed fact is appended as one list line under `## Notes` of the matching `profile/projects/<slug>.md` with the Edit tool before filling continues; a fact the human denies or does not know is dropped and the Slot is filled without it. No State edits an Overview. Done when every row of the fact table has a source and no question is open.

3. **Fill.** Fill the active Slots in template order, each by its Slot catalogue entry, the template's Slot notes for the plan Slot picked, and the voice rules, in our own words (no run of six words copied from the posting outside an ask header). The Hook Slot restates the Job's goal and constraint within the `hook` Slot `cap.chars` and opens with a phrase of understanding; with Flag `mandatory-keyword` triggered, the keyword appears nowhere in the Letter. The plan Slot carries the Safeguards mapped to it, in its own words; `billing` and `answers` carry theirs. When the plan costs more than the posted budget, `billing` states both figures and what the posted budget buys. Done when every active Slot has content or an explicit reason to render nothing, and every mapped demand and Safeguard is stated in its Slot.

4. **Render.** Assemble the plain text: the Profile `greeting.freelance` on line 1, a blank line, then the Slots in template order, one block each, one blank line between blocks. Headers from the Slot catalogue `headers` on their own line with no blank line before the content, replaced by the ask headers from step 1 for the Slots an ask points at, ask blocks in the Job's order after the plan block and the availability ask last, right before `closing`; the plan block's closing prose as its last lines; `sign_off` alone on the last line. Then read the text against the voice rules once as a whole: prose words under the template's `cap.prose_words` with header, list, and `answers` lines excluded, total under its `cap.total_words`, characters under its `cap.chars`, every Slot within its Slot catalogue `cap`, one list at most, only `https://` URLs, no banned phrase or pattern, no Markdown. Trim the longest prose Slot first. Done when the text passes that reading.

5. **Write `letter.md`** with the Write tool at `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/letter.md`: the Letter text alone, no frontmatter. The write hook blocks a file with frontmatter: drop it and write again. Done when the write went through.

6. **Question blocks.** Every question from step 1 is in `letter.md` under its header from step 1; nothing is answered outside `letter.md`. Done when each question has its answer in the file.

7. **Show.** Print, in this order: the plan Slot picked and its cues; the demand map, one line per demand as `demand -> Slot`, with the Safeguards placed; the Projects chosen and why, one line each; the fact table. No Letter here: the human sees it only after `verify` has passed it. Done when the four are on screen.

8. **Hand off.** Invoke `route-verify $ARGUMENTS` with the Skill tool, no question in between. Its report ends with the verified Letter. Done when `verify` has run.

## Employment route

The cover letter, from `$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR/autotriage/catalogue/templates/letter-employment.md` (its Slot notes and rule switches) and the Slot catalogue `Employment route` section. The same inputs, less the Flag catalogue: no employment Flag has a Safeguard Slot. Output: the body of `letter.md`, which the `version` State created empty next to `job.md`; the Version sent is the Candidate's `version.md` when it exists, else `resume/versions/general.md`.

1. **Version.** The guard output above names the Version sent and prints the summary the `intro` reads, with its source. Done when the summary is in hand.
2. **Demand map.** Walk the Job body for what the cover letter must answer: the `requirements` that `relevant_work` matches, and an explicit ask for terms (availability, notice, location, overlap). `terms` is active only with such an ask. Screening questions are form fields, never in the Letter: list them in step 6 and leave them to the human and the Answers in `profile/answers/`. A question asking for time with a Skill is marked in that list as one the human answers; never offer a number derived from the Projects' `start` and `end`. Done when `terms` has a decision.
3. **Facts.** `intro`: the summary's facts. `relevant_work`: stack and domains that match the Job's `requirements`, each traced to a Project's `keywords`, Overview, Bullets or Notes; no Project name, no date; a Gap is never named, the nearest thing done may stand as work done. A fact the Profile lacks goes to the human as in step 2 of the freelance route. Hold the fact table. Done when every row has a source.
4. **Fill and render.** Fill the active Slots in template order by the Slot catalogue, the template's Slot notes and rule switches, and the voice rules: `intro` is the summary in first person rephrased to the Job's keywords, a role title and `10+ years` allowed there only. Assemble the plain text: the Profile `greeting.employment` on line 1, a blank line, then one prose paragraph per Slot, one blank line between, no headers, no lists, `sign_off` alone on the last line. Count the characters, greeting to sign-off: within the template's `cap.chars`. Trim `relevant_work` first, never `closing` or `sign_off`. Done when the text is within the cap and passes a reading against the voice rules.
5. **Write `letter.md`** at `private/candidates/$ARGUMENTS/letter.md` with the Write tool: the Letter text alone, no frontmatter, replacing the empty file. Done when the write went through.
6. **Show.** Print the Version sent, the `terms` decision, the screening questions left for the form, and the fact table. No Letter here. Done when the four are on screen.
7. **Hand off.** Invoke `route-verify $ARGUMENTS` with the Skill tool, no question in between. Done when `verify` has run.
