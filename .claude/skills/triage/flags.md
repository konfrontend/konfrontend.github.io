# Flags

A Flag triggers on the meaning of its cues, not their wording. Red is a risk, green a good sign.

## Red

- `budget-scope-mismatch`: enterprise-size feature list with a small fixed price; $5-$100 placeholder budget; ambitious scope, tiny budget
- `vague-posting`: one-paragraph brief; enhance our web application using React and Node; no scope, no deliverables; a job ad with no responsibilities or requirements listed
- `non-technical-founder`: non-technical founder; I am not a developer; need someone to own the tech
- `partnership-anchor`: partner, not freelancer; more paid work after MVP; founding engineer; long-term partnership
- `client-code-self-assessment`: architecture is sound; hard thinking is done; codebase is clean; built in Replit/Lovable/Cursor, just needs finishing
- `external-deadline`: pilot launches in <month>; launch in the coming months; must be live by <date>
- `regulated-domain`: FERPA; HIPAA; PHI; student data; health data; legal content; GDPR sensitive data
- `moderate-gauntlet`: 3-5 targeted technical questions in the application
- `mandatory-keyword`: begin your proposal with <WORD>; start with the word; include <CODE> so I know you read this
- `burned-before`: previous developer went unresponsive; previous developer failed; estimates grew tenfold; we were let down
- `free-discovery`: include an architecture proposal; propose the DB design in your application; itemized estimate required in the proposal; describe your pipeline design
- `subjective-acceptance`: needs to look great; pixel-perfect; your creative call; until we are happy
- `control-heavy-environment`: mandatory sandboxed workstation; remote-desktop only; monitored access; time tracker with screenshots required
- `heavy-vetting`: references will be called; CV + LinkedIn + GitHub + portfolio mandatory; we will independently verify
- `fixed-price-unbounded`: production-ready as the only acceptance criterion; scalable, high-quality; feature list over 8 modules with a fixed budget
- `urgency-48h`: URGENT; 24H; event tomorrow; need it today
- `excessive-gauntlet`: 10+ application questions; video recording required; take-home task for a small job
- `hostile-tone`: ALL-CAPS plus complaints; DO NOT APPLY IF; prohibition lists; threats of rejection; no time-wasters
- `unrealistic-mechanics`: build it in 1 hour on Zoom while I watch; should take you 20 minutes
- `fixed-urgent-vague`: urgent, fixed budget, just make it work
- `off-platform`: contact me outside Upwork; Telegram/WhatsApp me; requirements unrelated to skill (gender, nationality, religion)
- `unmet-constraint`: US-only; must be based in <country>; citizens only; on-site; hybrid; relocate; must be available <hours> in <timezone> with no overlap possible; a required license, security clearance or degree stated as required; any location, identity, credential or working-arrangement requirement Profile identity.constraints cannot meet

## Green

- `paid-first-milestone`: paid scoping phase; paid audit first; paid architecture assignment; shortlist then paid assessment
- `assets-provided`: PRD attached; designs in Figma; wireframes ready; spec document provided
- `team-in-place`: our CTO; existing dev team; you will work with our backend developer
