# ATS title matching, headline, phone and education: what the vendors document

Research notes, 2026-10-12. Primary sources only (vendor docs, help centers, product pages, first-party blogs). Secondary-only claims are listed under "Confidence and gaps".

## Short answers

1. **Exact job-title matching.** A scored check in Jobscan (third of five match-rate priorities). Not a scored check in Resume Worded (writing advice only). Greenhouse and Lever document no automatic ranking: Greenhouse states outright that it does not score or rank applications; both offer exact-keyword search over parsed resumes. Workday (HiredScore grades, Skills Match Score), iCIMS (search Rank column), Oracle Recruiting (Suggested Candidates, AI matching ratings) and SmartRecruiters (Winston Match) do document scoring. Only SmartRecruiters names "job title relevance" as a scored dimension; Oracle's "Profile" criterion considers "the title and description in the candidate's profile". Taleo Enterprise ranks by prescreening answers, not resume text.
2. **Headline "Full-stack" vs "Senior Frontend".** No vendor documents the resume headline changing keyword weights. Every documented scorer takes its term list and weights from the posting (Jobscan: skills "from the job listing"; SmartRecruiters: job title, description, qualifications fields; HiredScore: required and preferred qualifications). A full-stack headline adds the words "full-stack" and nothing else to the match.
3. **Phone number.** No documented ATS or parser fails on a missing phone. Textkernel reports it as a quality code and says that "does not indicate that the Parser failed". SmartRecruiters states phone numbers are not used in the match score. Jobscan checks that contact details sit in the body, not header/footer; the five match-rate priorities do not include contact info. Resume Worded's advice calls the phone a must-have but documents no score effect.
4. **Education.** Jobscan weighs education only "when an advanced degree is included in the job description". Textkernel reports a missing Education section as code 414, not a failure. Affinda returns education as an array; Daxtra and HireAbility document no section as mandatory. No parser doc describes an absent section as a parse failure.
5. Dates per source below. Secondary-only items in the last section.

## Evidence

### Q1 Exact job-title matching

- Jobscan, resume scanner FAQ (https://www.jobscan.co/resume-scanner, undated, footer 2026): "Your match rate is based on these five priorities, in order:" hard skills; "Education level (only when an advanced degree is included in the job description)"; job title; soft skills; other keywords. Same list on https://www.jobscan.co/ and https://www.jobscan.co/jobscan-tutorial. The scanner page also says: "when you submit your resume to a job, the ATS doesn't score your resume."
- Jobscan tutorial (https://www.jobscan.co/jobscan-tutorial, image alt "updated March 2025"): "resumes that had an exact job title match had an interview rate 3.5 times higher than ones that did not." Advice: change the title in work experience to the posting's if the role was similar; otherwise put it in the summary.
- Jobscan blog, ATS resume (https://www.jobscan.co/blog/ats-resume/, 2026-04-08): "Jobscan analyzed nearly one million job searches and found that resumes that contained the job title received 10.6 times more interview invitations". Two different multipliers from the same vendor, no methodology published for either.
- Jobscan match-rate post (https://www.jobscan.co/blog/what-jobscan-match-rate-should-i-aim-for/, 2022-05-13): recommends 80%; the "matching algorithm is based off of top applicant tracking systems". Scanner page and tutorial say 75%.
- Resume Worded, Targeted Resume (https://resumeworded.com/targeted-resume, undated): the tool "calculates a comparison score" ("Relevancy Score") by "comparing your resume to the job description and finding important keywords you missed"; below 80 means "missing important skills". No job-title check named. Help center (https://help.resumeworded.com/article/33-how-is-my-score-calculated, 2020-09-14): overall score "weights specific checks differently"; no job-title check named. Header advice (https://resumeworded.com/resume-header-key-advice, "3 months ago" as of 2026-10): "This should be the title of the job you're applying to, not the job you currently hold", because "Some resume screeners search your resume for keywords". Advice, not a scored check.
- Greenhouse, Talent Filtering (https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/27104809835291-Talent-Filtering, updated 2026-03-02): "the keyword from your search must exactly match the keyword in the application"; preferred terms OR, required terms AND; results are filtered, no score or rank described.
- Greenhouse, candidate blog (https://my.greenhouse.com/blogs/what-really-happens-after-you-apply-for-a-job, 2025-01-08): "AI doesn't score or rank applications, nor does it make any decisions about whether or not you move forward"; applications "reviewed by real people, one by one, in the order received"; AI only suggests search keywords from the job description.
- Greenhouse, full-text search (https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/115004600186-Search-resumes-for-keywords, updated 2022-06-06): keyword search with snippets; no relevance ranking described.
- Lever, searching the database (https://help.lever.co/hc/en-us/articles/20087317030685-Searching-the-database-for-candidates): search covers names and "any parseable content attached to candidate profiles, including resumes, notes, and feedback forms"; `Resume:` filter limits matches to parsed resume text; advanced search filters on parsed job titles, companies, schools. No score or ranking described. Page returns 401 to fetch tools; wording taken from search-engine excerpts of the article.
- Lever blog on AI screening (https://www.lever.co/blog/ai-resume-screening, 2026-09-08): generic guidance; says exact-term reliance may "miss candidates who describe equivalent experience using different language". No Lever scoring mechanics described.
- Workday HiredScore grades (https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/recruiter-productivity-/reference--candidate-grades.html, updated 2023-06-23): A = "meets all basic (required) qualifications, most preferred qualifications" and an average match above a threshold; B = all basic; C = most basic; D = "does not meet most basic qualifications". Job title not named as an input.
- Workday Candidate Skills Match (https://doc.workday.com/admin-guide/en-us/human-capital-management/recruiting/candidates/candidate-skills-match/ave1604091571278.html, updated 2026-03-13): Skills Match Score "based on the similarity of skills between the job application and job requisition"; "We plan to retire this functionality in a future release."
- iCIMS Searching and Reporting FAQ (https://community.icims.com/articles/FAQ/Searching-Reporting-FAQ, undated): "The Rank column's percentage is based on where the skill set that was searched for falls in the resume" (higher up, higher) "and how many times that skill set appears within the resume." Position and frequency of the searched term, not the job title. iCIMS glossary (https://www.icims.com/glossary/applicant-tracking-system-ats/, undated, marketing): ATSs "rank candidates based on criteria, including years of experience, the relevance of keywords and prior role".
- Oracle Taleo Enterprise 21c, keyword search (https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/taleo-enterprise/21c/otrcg/c-advancedsearchkeywords.html): AND by default, OR/NOT supported, "Keyword search is considered to provide exact matches"; no ranking. ACE alert (https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/taleo-enterprise/21c/otrcg/c-acecandidatealert.html): "identifies top candidates based on their responses to the competencies and questions in the Prescreening section". Resume text and job title not inputs.
- Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Suggested Candidates (https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/talent-management/faush/understand-suggested-candidates.html, undated): four criteria at "0-3 stars indicating the relative match"; "Profile: Considers the title and description in the candidate's profile." AI matching ratings (https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/talent-management/farqa/evaluate-candidate-applications-using-ai-matching-ratings.html, undated): "ratings on a scale of 0 to 5 for these categories: Education, Experience, Skills, Profile", from "parsed resume information".
- SmartRecruiters, AI recruiting page (https://prod-ohio.smartrecruiters.com/recruiting-software/artificial-intelligence-recruitment-tools/, titled 2020, footer 2026): SmartAssistant match score; "Scoring and recommendations require profile data from candidates." Talent matching page (https://www.smartrecruiters.com/recruiting-software/talent-matching/, ~2026-10, returns 403 to fetch tools; wording from search excerpts): Winston Match scores "skills match, work experience, education and certifications, career progression, and job title relevance", "each dimension is scored using separate models and then ensembled into a single weighted score".

### Q2 Headline and keyword weighting

- Jobscan home FAQ (https://www.jobscan.co/): "It compares hard skills, soft skills, and industry buzzwords from the job listing to your resume." Match report video (https://www.jobscan.co/video-resume-match-report): skills weighted per posting; "Predicted skills do not affect your match rate since they weren't technically part of the job description you entered." Headline guidance (https://www.jobscan.co/blog/ats-resume/, 2026-04-08) treats the headline as a keyword location for the job title only.
- SmartRecruiters AI whitepaper (https://ta.smartrecruiters.com/rs/664-NIC-529/images/SmartRecruiters-AI-Whitepaper.pdf, ~2024; PDF too large for fetch tools, wording from search excerpts): "Only data from the Job Title, Job Description, and Qualifications fields on a job are considered"; CV skills mapped to ESCO and compared.
- HiredScore grades (above): inputs are required and preferred qualifications of the requisition.
- No vendor page found where the resume headline changes which keywords count or how much. Keyword sets come from the posting side in every documented scorer.

### Q3 Phone number

- Textkernel Tx Platform v10 parser output (https://developer.textkernel.com/tx-platform/v10/resume-parser/overview/parser-output/, undated, v10): code 212 "Indicates if no phone number was found to contact the candidate."; code 441 "neither an email address nor a phone number were found"; "Reporting that fact does not indicate that the Parser failed."
- SmartRecruiters AI page (above): "The SmartAssistant algorithms do not analyze nor use any personal candidate data"; whitepaper excerpt lists "phone number" among excluded data.
- Jobscan scanner page (above): "Checks that contact information in the body of the resume and not in the header/footer." Formatting check; contact info is not one of the five match-rate priorities. Tutorial: "if our scanner can't pick up things like your name and contact info ... an ATS won't be able to either."
- Resume Worded header advice (above): phone is one of "four things your resume header must include". Advice page; no score effect documented.
- Affinda data fields (https://docs.affinda.com/resumes/data-extracted, undated): `phoneNumber` returned as an array; unparsed fields come back `null` in the sample. No failure mode for a missing phone.

### Q4 Education

- Jobscan (above): education counts "only when an advanced degree is included in the job description"; the scanner "Reviews job title, years of experience match ... and education match." Tutorial: builder lets you leave Education blank and continue.
- Textkernel v10 (above): code 414 "Indicates that an EDUCATION section was not found."; 416 education "found but had to be calculated as a section"; 151 flags headers not on a separate line. All quality codes, none a failure.
- Affinda (above): `education` is an array of objects; missing data appears as `null`/empty, no error described.
- Daxtra CVX integration page (https://cvxdemo.daxtra.com/cvx/, undated, REST v1): on error "instead of the "Resume" element, DaXtra Parser returns a "CSERROR" element"; no section listed as required.
- HireAbility parsers (https://www.hireability.com/hireability-resume-job-parsers/, undated): "any subset of the output tags can be returned on request"; summaries include "highest level of education"; no handling of absent sections documented.
- Oracle AI matching ratings (above): "When relevant information is available in the resume, Generative AI evaluates: Education, Experience, Skills." Absent data lowers that category, no failure.

## Confidence and gaps

- High: Jobscan's five priorities and the "ATS doesn't score" sentence (three Jobscan pages agree). Greenhouse's no-ranking statement and exact-match filtering. Textkernel quality codes. HiredScore grade definitions. Taleo keyword search and ACE prescreening.
- Jobscan's 3.5x and 10.6x job-title figures are vendor claims with no published method; the two numbers conflict.
- Jobscan's Taleo post (https://www.jobscan.co/blog/taleo-popular-ats-ranks-job-applications/, 2026-04-10) describes Oracle Recruiting Cloud's Suggested Candidates stars, not Taleo Enterprise; Oracle's own pages confirm the stars, so the claim holds but the product label is loose.
- Lever help article, iCIMS community FAQ, SmartRecruiters talent-matching page and whitepaper: pages blocked fetch tools (401/403/size); wording comes from search-engine excerpts of the vendor pages, not from reading the page. Treat the SmartRecruiters "job title relevance" dimension and "Only data from the Job Title, Job Description, and Qualifications fields" as secondary-grade until read directly.
- Resume Worded publishes no list of its checks beyond "30+ checks"; absence of a job-title check is inferred from the pages read, not stated.
- No vendor publishes weights for any score. No source at all, primary or secondary, documents the resume headline altering keyword weighting.
- Taleo Enterprise, Lever and Greenhouse: no primary doc on parse-failure behavior for missing sections; only the four parser vendors above were checked.
