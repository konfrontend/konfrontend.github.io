# Professional profile

One person's Profile, the resumes made from it, and the triage of the Jobs it is sent for. The Profile is the source of truth; a Version and a Letter derive from it, never the other way round. Say resume, never CV.

## Language

### Profile

**Profile**:
The person's data every Version and Letter derives from: the Projects with their descriptions, Keywords, Bullets and notes, the answers to recurring questions, the contact facts, the voice and the Route's settings.
_Avoid_: user profile, persona, data folder, master

**Version**:
One resume, written by the person: a title, a summary, the Projects and Bullets picked for it, each picked Project's Keywords, and the Skills lines. The descriptions and Bullets it shows are the Projects' own text.
_Avoid_: draft, template, selection, CV, CV file, resume file

**Project**:
One job, engagement or personal project, done or ongoing; an ongoing Project has no end date.
_Avoid_: past job, company, client, position, experience, portfolio item

**Bullet**:
One achievement of a Project: a heading that names it and the claim in the person's prose. A Version picks a Bullet by its heading and shows the claim.
_Avoid_: highlight, point, achievement, lead, lead line

**Keyword**:
The one spelling of a technology or practice the person holds, as a posting or an ATS would match it: the same in a Project, in a Version and in a Candidate's requirements. A required item no Keyword matches keeps the posting's spelling.
_Avoid_: tag, tech name, alias, term

**Gap**:
An item a Job required that no Keyword names, named as the posting spells it. Exists only because a Job required it; never declared and never written into a Letter or a Version.
_Avoid_: missing skill, weakness, hole, target

### Jobs

**Job**:
One posting, freelance or employment: its free-form text plus the parameters its Route requires.
_Avoid_: posting, listing, gig, opportunity

**Candidate**:
A Job being pursued through a Route, recorded as one folder: the Job and the Letter. Becomes a Project when won.
_Avoid_: application, lead, intake

**Outcome**:
The furthest point a pursued Candidate reached: sent, interview or offer, set by the person. A missing next step is the negative outcome.
_Avoid_: status, stage, result

### Route

**Route**:
A fixed sequence of steps for one kind of work: the freelance route or the employment route.
_Avoid_: pipeline, flow, workflow, state machine

**Guard**:
The condition a step of a Route needs before it runs: the previous step's output exists and is valid, and the Route's preconditions hold.
_Avoid_: gate, precondition check, validation step

**Checkpoint**:
The pause after triage where the person reads the scores and Flags and answers go or no-go; the Verdict is written then and nothing before.
_Avoid_: approval, review step, gate

**Verdict**:
Whether a Job is pursued: the go or no-go the person confirmed at the Checkpoint. The scores, the Flags and the recommendation behind it are session output.
_Avoid_: decision, recommendation, result, assessment

**Flag**:
One named risk signal in a Job, with a level 1–5; a green Flag lowers the risk, a stop Flag forces a no-go.
_Avoid_: red flag, warning, concern

**Letter**:
The pasteable text a Route writes: a proposal on the freelance route, a cover letter on the employment route.
_Avoid_: bid, application, pitch

**Slot**:
One named part of a Letter template, called by its name plus Slot.
_Avoid_: section, block, fragment
