# Professional profile

One person's Profile, the resumes made from it, and the triage of the Jobs it is sent for. The Profile is the source of truth; a Version and a Letter draw from it, never the other way round. Say resume, never CV.

## Language

### Profile

**Profile**:
The person's data every Version and Letter draws from: the Projects with their Stories, the Skills they prove, the Answers, the contact facts, the voice and the Route's settings.
_Avoid_: user profile, persona, data folder

**Master**:
Everything the Profile can show on a resume in one document: every Story of every Project in resume order, with the contact line and every Skill. Generated, never edited; a Version selects from it.
_Avoid_: full resume, draft, pool, resume source

**Version**:
One resume: a selection of Stories by key per Project, with its own Skills lines and summary. The general Version is behind the public PDF; a tailored Version is one Candidate's copy of it.
_Avoid_: draft, template, CV, CV file, resume file

**Project**:
One job, engagement or personal project, done or ongoing; an ongoing Project has no end date.
_Avoid_: past job, company, client, position, experience, portfolio item

**Project file**:
The full record of one Project: its meta, the Keywords it proves, and its Stories.
_Avoid_: pool, inventory, log, project log

**Story**:
One piece of a Project's work, with a key that never changes: its Bullet and the Facts behind it.
_Avoid_: entry, item, anecdote, case, highlight

**Bullet**:
The lead line of a Story: one line of polished prose shown verbatim in the Master and in any Version that selects the Story.
_Avoid_: highlight, point, achievement, lead

**Fact**:
One verifiable claim inside a Story, such as the client, the stack, a number or an outcome, that the Bullet rests on and a Letter may cite.
_Avoid_: detail, data point, context

**Skill**:
One technology or domain practice some Project proves, named by one canonical Keyword. Years of experience and spoken languages are not Skills.
_Avoid_: technology, stack item, competency, capability, ledger row

**Keyword**:
The one spelling of a Skill, as a posting or an ATS would match it: the same in a Project file, a Version's Skills lines and a Candidate's requirements.
_Avoid_: tag, tech name, alias, term

**Gap**:
A Skill a Job required that no Project proves. Exists only because a Job required it; counted, never declared.
_Avoid_: missing skill, weakness, hole, target

**Answer**:
The person's hand-written reply to one recurring question a Job may ask. A Letter cites it word for word or tailors it; the Answers together are what the voice is tuned by.
_Avoid_: snippet, canned answer, calibration

### Jobs

**Job**:
One posting, freelance or employment: its free-form text plus the parameters its Route requires.
_Avoid_: posting, listing, gig, opportunity

**Candidate**:
A Job being pursued, moving through a Route. Becomes a Project when won.
_Avoid_: application, lead, intake

### Route

**Route**:
A fixed sequence of States for one kind of work: the freelance route or the employment route.
_Avoid_: pipeline, flow, workflow, state machine

**State**:
One step of a Route and the one output it produces.
_Avoid_: stage, phase, step

**Guard**:
The condition a State needs before it runs: the previous State's output exists and is valid, and the Route's preconditions hold.
_Avoid_: gate, precondition check, validation step

**Verdict**:
Whether a Job is pursued: go, no-go or ask, with the scores and triggered Flags behind it.
_Avoid_: decision, result, assessment

**Flag**:
One named risk signal in a Job, with a level 1–5; a green Flag lowers the risk, a stop Flag forces a no-go.
_Avoid_: red flag, warning, concern

**Letter**:
The pasteable text a Route writes: a proposal on the freelance route, a cover letter on the employment route.
_Avoid_: bid, application, pitch

**Slot**:
One named part of a Letter template, called by its name plus Slot.
_Avoid_: section, block, fragment
