# Professional profile

One person's Profile, the resumes made from it, and the triage of the Jobs it is sent for. The Profile is the source of truth; a Version and a Letter derive from it, never the other way round. Say resume, never CV.

## Language

### Profile

**Profile**:
The person's data every Version and Letter derives from: the Projects with their Stories, Bullets and Notes, the Skills they prove, the Answers, the contact facts, the voice and the Route's settings.
_Avoid_: user profile, persona, data folder, master

**Version**:
One resume as its PDF shows it: a title, a summary, the Skills lines and the Bullets it shows, generated from the Project files. The general Version is behind the public PDF; a tailored Version is one Candidate's own and may carry Variants.
_Avoid_: draft, template, selection, CV, CV file, resume file

**Plan**:
The ranked choice a Version is generated from: its title, summary, Skills lines and the Bullets in order of worth, a Variant's text where one is wanted. Passed, never stored; the Version is the record.
_Avoid_: selection, outline, config

**Project**:
One job, engagement or personal project, done or ongoing; an ongoing Project has no end date.
_Avoid_: past job, company, client, position, experience, portfolio item

**Project file**:
The full record of one Project: its meta, the Keywords it proves, its Stories with their canonical Bullets, the Bullets with no Story yet, and its Notes.
_Avoid_: pool, inventory, log, project log

**Story**:
A case study of one piece of a Project's work, in prose: the problem, the solution, the outcome. Written by the person, never shown on a resume; its canonical Bullets sit under it.
_Avoid_: entry, item, anecdote, case, highlight

**Bullet**:
The one- or two-line summary of a Story that a resume shows, under a key that never changes. Written from the Story by the Bullet rules and reviewed by the person; a Story holds one or more, each on a different achievement. A Bullet with no Story yet is valid until its Story is written.
_Avoid_: highlight, point, achievement, lead, lead line

**Variant**:
A Bullet reworded for one tailored Version under the key of a canonical Bullet of the same Story: it reorders and re-emphasises, never adds a claim. Never in the general Version.
_Avoid_: tailored bullet, rewrite, override

**Notes**:
Raw material for a Project's future Stories, kept in its Project file. A Letter may cite them; a Version never shows them.
_Avoid_: facts, context, backlog, scratch

**Fact**:
Any claim in a Story, a Bullet or Notes, verified against the work when written and trusted afterwards. A Letter cites it; a Variant may not invent one.
_Avoid_: detail, data point, context

**Skill**:
One technology or domain practice some Project proves, named by one canonical Keyword. Years of experience and spoken languages are not Skills.
_Avoid_: technology, stack item, competency, capability, ledger row

**Keyword**:
The one spelling of a Skill, as a posting or an ATS would match it: the same in a Project file, a Version's Skills lines and a Candidate's requirements. A required item no Project matches keeps the posting's spelling.
_Avoid_: tag, tech name, alias, term

**Gap**:
A Skill-shaped item a Job required that no Project proves, named as the posting spells it. Exists only because a Job required it; counted by the Gap print, never declared and never written into a Letter or a Version.
_Avoid_: missing skill, weakness, hole, target

**Gap print**:
The table of every Gap and every proved Skill a Job asked for, with how many Candidates asked, no-go Candidates included.
_Avoid_: ledger, skill ledger, demand report

**Answer**:
The person's hand-written reply to one recurring question a Job may ask. A Letter cites it word for word or tailors it; the Answers together are what the voice is tuned by.
_Avoid_: snippet, canned answer, calibration

### Jobs

**Job**:
One posting, freelance or employment: its free-form text plus the parameters its Route requires.
_Avoid_: posting, listing, gig, opportunity

**Candidate**:
A Job being pursued through a Route, recorded as one folder: the Job, the Letter, and a tailored Version when one was made. Becomes a Project when won.
_Avoid_: application, lead, intake

**Outcome**:
The furthest point a pursued Candidate reached: sent, interview or offer, set by the person. A missing next step is the negative outcome.
_Avoid_: status, stage, result

**Tracker**:
The table of every Candidate and where it stands: its Route, its Verdict, its Outcome.
_Avoid_: pipeline view, dashboard, board

### Route

**Route**:
A fixed sequence of States for one kind of work: the freelance route or the employment route.
_Avoid_: pipeline, flow, workflow, state machine

**State**:
One step of a Route and the one output it produces.
_Avoid_: stage, phase, step

**Guard**:
The condition a State needs before it runs: the previous State's output exists and is valid, and the Route's preconditions hold.
_Avoid_: gate, precondition check, validation step

**Checkpoint**:
The pause after triage where the person reads the scores and Flags and answers go or no-go; the Verdict is written then and nothing before.
_Avoid_: approval, review step, gate

**Verdict**:
Whether a Job is pursued: the go or no-go the person confirmed at the Checkpoint. The scores, the Flags and the recommendation behind it are session output.
_Avoid_: decision, recommendation, result, assessment

**Flag**:
One named risk signal in a Job, with a level 1–5; a green Flag lowers the risk, a stop Flag forces a no-go.
_Avoid_: red flag, warning, concern

**Letter**:
The pasteable text a Route writes: a proposal on the freelance route, a cover letter on the employment route.
_Avoid_: bid, application, pitch

**Slot**:
One named part of a Letter template, called by its name plus Slot.
_Avoid_: section, block, fragment
