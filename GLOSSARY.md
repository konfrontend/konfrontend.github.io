# CV workspace

The source of one person's CV and the facts it is built from. The CV is rebuilt from a pool of facts per job, not edited in place.

## Language

**Highlights Pool**:
The durable, unlimited record of CV-worthy facts for one job, grouped by theme. One per project.
_Avoid_: notes, inventory, log

**Highlight**:
One fact in a pool, written as a single bullet.
_Avoid_: achievement, claim, item

**Theme**:
A named group of highlights inside a pool, such as "Browser extension". The unit of approval and of deepening.
_Avoid_: section, category, area

**Project**:
One job, engagement or personal project with its own pool and its own entry in the draft.
_Avoid_: company, client, position

**Scan**:
A session that reads one repository and proposes highlights for its pool. Runs in gather mode or deepen mode.
_Avoid_: audit, review, extraction

**Gather**:
The scan mode that proposes new highlights across all themes.
_Avoid_: discover, collect

**Deepen**:
The scan mode that revisits one theme and replaces or extends it.
_Avoid_: refine, expand, update

**Clue**:
A fact the user states about a project in chat, during a scan or a port. A clue is true and is never dropped; the scan looks for evidence that makes it stronger, the port writes it into the pool before the bullet.
_Avoid_: hint, guidance, suggestion

**Rank**:
The agreed strength of a highlight against the others in its project, A strongest to C. Decides bullet order, merges and cuts.
_Avoid_: priority, weight, score, tier (that word belongs to keywords)

**Draft**:
The CV document itself, from which the PDF is rendered.
_Avoid_: template, resume source, CV file

**Port**:
Moving hand-picked highlights from a pool into the draft as compact bullets.
_Avoid_: sync, import, copy

**Skills line**:
The comma-separated keyword list that closes a pool. Port reads it to choose which keywords must surface inside the bullets.
_Avoid_: tech stack, tags

**Bullet**:
One line under a project in the draft, rewritten from one or more highlights during a port.
_Avoid_: highlight, point, achievement

**Guidelines**:
The written rules for highlights, bullets and keywords that every scan and port follows.
_Avoid_: best practices, style guide
